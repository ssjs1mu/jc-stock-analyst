What this does

Enter a symbol, choose the exchange and press Run deep scan. It automatically scans the last 15 · 30 · 45 · 60 · 75 · 90 sessions, builds intraday and delivery levels, finds the common days and repeating moves, matches the big moves with news, tests an astro (Moon) angle, and forecasts the next 3 sessions — today's close, then each next open and close with a minimum–maximum range — together with a walk-forward backtest that shows how well the method really worked on days it had not seen.
Honest framing: markets are noisy. Where the backtest shows no edge, the page says so and shows a wide range around "no change" instead of a precise-looking number. Research aid, not advice or a guarantee.
